# Public portfolio publication contract (draft v1)

The current public explorer's version-1 listing and geography files remain unchanged.
The first multi-city deliverable is a source-cited case study and reviewed **aggregate**
research evidence. Include market/snapshot dates, source and CC BY 4.0 links, modifications,
cohort selection, questions, configurations, split hashes, support counts, metrics, errors,
uncertainty method and limitations. Mark planned work and insufficient support plainly.

No source archive, full source table, host/reviewer identity, review text, description,
secret, model binary, private split membership or address-level claim is published. Any
listing-level aggregate review score/count requires a separately versioned allowlist,
privacy/granularity review and tests before it enters the site. Suppress or combine
examples that could expose people through thin cells. Source-derived fields must not be
presented as confirmed bookings, realized earnings, future renovation outcomes or legal
eligibility. The old model's failed release gate remains visible and public predictions
remain withheld pending a separate approved evaluation.

An optional authenticated reviewer demo does not change the publication review or license
requirements. Audit every reachable production, preview and static-asset URL before any
access claim. Do not alter the existing Pages/Worker/DNS setup or activate paid services
without a separate reviewed issue and authorization.
